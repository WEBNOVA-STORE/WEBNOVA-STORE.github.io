Questions

Before you order

Do I need to buy a domain?

No. Your site can go live on a free link that stays online 24/7. If you later want your own .com domain, we can connect it — the domain would be bought in your own name.

How do customers order from me?

Message on WhatsApp first. You see a preview of your site on a private link, and only after payment is the site handed over — as files, or deployed to your own account so you fully own it.

Will it work well on phones?

Yes — every site is designed mobile-first and tested at phone size. Animations use lightweight code, so pages stay fast on mobile Chrome.

How long does it take?

It depends on the package and how fast you send your content (name, photos, prices). The timeline is confirmed on WhatsApp before work starts.

Can I update the site later?

Yes. Small text and photo changes can be done anytime — just message on WhatsApp and we will update and re-publish your site.
